FONTLOG for Everygen Neue
=========================

Everygen Neue is a modified version of Overused Grotesk by Bao Nguyen
(RandomMaerks), released under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 (see OFL.txt).
Overused Grotesk declares no Reserved Font Name. Everygen Neue is a renamed
derivative and may not be sold by itself.

Copyright (c) 2023-2025, Bao Nguyen/RandomMaerks.
Copyright (c) 2026 Everygen (modifications).

Every outline and OpenType substitution in these files comes from Overused
Grotesk or was produced by the transformations listed below. Since v0.300 the
kerning is computed by Everygen from these outlines (items 29, 37, 38); no
kerning value is inherited or copied. The pnum substitution (item 41) is the
reverse of Overused's own tnum. No outline, point, kerning, hinting or feature
data from any other font was used.

Since v0.501 no per-glyph or per-pair number from any other font is used
either. Some targets were chosen by measuring a reference typeface, and only
as font-wide, class or set aggregates: stem widths, stroke contrast and bar
thickness, vertical metrics, x-height, descender depth, class-mean advances
(item 47), class-average sidebearings (item 24), punctuation class gaps
(items 22 and 25), pair-category means and spreads (item 37), figure-set
means (items 39, 40, 42, 48) and the gap between the figures and the math
class (item 53). Versions 0.200 to 0.401 had set 20 letters to the
reference's own advance width (items 12 and 26) and $ and % to its ink width
for each sign (item 42). Versions 0.400 to 0.500 also set the figure-facing
side of $, % and + from that one sign's gap to the figures (items 42 and 48).
v0.501 removes all of that.

ChangeLog
---------

2026-10-20  v0.501  Fixes found in v0.5 verification

53. Signs next to figures. Items 42 and 48 set the side of $ that meets a
   figure, the side of % that meets a figure, and both sides of the math
   signs from the reference's mean gap between that one sign (for the math
   signs, + alone) and the ten figures. Each of those numbers describes one
   glyph of the reference, so they are deleted. $ and % keep Overused's own
   sidebearings after the figure ink factor of item 48. The math signs take
   one shift per side for the class, from the mean gap between the ten
   figures and all five signs + - = x / (50 pairs per side).
54. Version 0.501 in nameID 3 and 5 and head.fontRevision.

2026-10-20  v0.500  Class widths; no per-glyph reference numbers

47. Widths by class. Items 12 and 26 are withdrawn. Their per-glyph advance
   targets for W M w f X G t R K Q k C & @ Y s T O e H were the reference's
   own advances and are deleted from the build. Every letter a-z and A-Z
   whose ink is more than a bare stem, plus & and @, now belongs to one of
   ten classes set by how the letter is built: lowercase straight (h m n u),
   round (a b c d e g o p q s), diagonal (k v w x y z), narrow (f j r t);
   capital straight (B D H N P R U), round (C G O Q S), diagonal
   (A K V X Y Z), wide (M W), narrow (E F J L T); and & @. Each class takes
   one width factor per weight, fitted so the class's mean advance equals the
   reference's class mean. The factor scales the ink with the stem
   compensation of item 12, so stems keep their weight and sidebearings
   stay. f and t stretch to the right of the stem (item 26). i, l and I
   keep their width. Each letter's alternates take its class factor, and
   its single-outline accented forms take the letter's own scale; composites
   follow as in item 12. Regular factors: lowercase 1.001 / 0.998 / 1.000 /
   1.133, capitals 1.005 / 1.036 / 1.037 / 0.928 / 0.991, & @ 0.949.
48. $ and %. The per-sign ink widths of item 42 are deleted. Both signs now
   take the default figure set's ink factor (item 39) on Overused's drawing.
   The side that meets a figure is still set from the mean gap to the ten
   figures.
49. Spacing of G and a. Item 4 had exempted the right sides of G and a from
   respacing because one per-glyph reference sidebearing matched. That
   exemption is gone. a's right side keeps the classifier's result, and G's
   right side is classed as round, which is the shape of its body.
50. Item 24 spacing, the kerning of items 29 and 37-38, the figures, dots and
   math signs of items 39-46 and the italics of item 31 are recomputed on
   the new widths with the same rules and the same aggregate targets.
51. The build is one script (build_v05.py). The older build scripts can no
   longer produce fonts.
52. Version 0.500 in nameID 3 and 5 and head.fontRevision.

2026-10-20  v0.401  Fix found in v0.4 verification

45. Math signs. Item 42 scaled + - = x / +- < > horizontally only, so
   + was taller than wide (470 by 523 at Regular) and the arms of x were
   no longer at 45 degrees. The class factor of item 42 now applies to both
   axes about each sign's centre, with stem compensation in x (item 12) and
   the same compensation for the horizontal bars in y, so bars and stems
   keep their weight. The tabular forms of these signs take the same factor
   and stay centred on the tabular advance.
46. Version 0.401 in nameID 3 and 5 and head.fontRevision.

2026-10-20  v0.400  Diagonal kerning, ? and !, figures

37. Diagonal pairs. The kerner (item 29) took each side's looseness next to
   neutral letters out of the kern. For a diagonal side most of that is its
   open triangle, so pairs like A V, Y o, A T and V a kept both triangles and
   sat 40-50 units loose. In ten pair categories (diagonal against diagonal,
   round or open, by case, plus one category for interlocking pairs such as
   A V, L V, A T and A v) a diagonal side now carries no bias. Each category
   has one allowance and one factor, fitted so the category's mean and spread
   of pair gaps equal the reference's for that weight. In these categories a
   kern may also open a pair, by at most 30. Every collision guard of items
   34 and 35 still applies. Italics use their upright's fitted values.
38. Round and open letters before ? and !. Their kern now makes the gap
   equal to this font's own gap between its straight-sided letters (n h m u,
   H N I M U) and the same mark. No reference value is involved.
39. Default figures stay proportional, as in the reference. One horizontal
   scale for the set (with stem compensation, as item 12) brings their mean
   ink width to the reference's mean; each figure's advance is pulled toward
   the set mean so the spread matches too; one shift per side sets the mean
   sidebearings, and a shared correction makes the mean gap between two
   figures equal the reference's mean (which includes its figure kerning).
   zero.slsh follows the set.
40. Tabular figures (tnum) take the reference's tabular advance, 600 at every
   weight, with one scale and one shift per side for the set. The tabular
   math signs and hyphen drawn at the old tabular width are recentred on 600.
41. pnum added: the reverse of every tnum substitution.
42. (The $ and % part is withdrawn in v0.500, item 48.) $ and % take one horizontal scale each, sized to the reference's ink
   width for that sign; the side that meets a figure is set from the
   reference's mean gap to the ten figures. + - = x / +- < > share one scale
   and two side shifts, set the same way from the class means.
43. Dots next to figures. The right side of period and comma, and both sides
   of colon and semicolon, are set so the kerned mean gaps between figures
   and these classes equal the reference's (9:16, 1.5, 1,000).
44. Version 0.400 in nameID 3 and 5 and head.fontRevision.

2026-10-20  v0.301  Fixes found in v0.3 verification

34. Kerning collision guard. The kerner read glyph profiles every 10 units,
   so a bar that slipped between two scanlines went unseen: at Light the
   F arm met the waist of a (F a kerned -140, the inks overlapped). A
   negative kern must now also clear the minimum gap on rows read every
   unit within 5 units of each scanline. This only moves a kern toward 0,
   never opens a pair. Changes in the basic Latin set: Fa -140 to -82
   (Light), c-hyphen -110 to -40 (Regular), C-hyphen -110 to -45
   (Semibold), plus some accented members.
35. Punctuation next to punctuation. Since item 25 the inner sides of the
   brackets are tight, so () [] and similar pairs met at the tips. Any
   punctuation class pair closer than the kerner's minimum gap is opened to
   it (about 20 class pairs per font, for example () +36 to +42).
36. Version 0.301 in nameID 3 and 5 and head.fontRevision.

2026-10-20  v0.300  Spacing, kerning and italics

24. Spacing system. Each side of a-z and A-Z belongs to one of eight classes
   (straight, round, diagonal, open; lowercase and capitals). Each side first
   moves halfway toward the value that gives its class equal white area once
   open counters are clipped 80 units deep (at most 6 units; the right sides of
   a, G and Q keep their value), then the whole class shifts so its average
   visible sidebearing equals the reference class average for that weight.
   Other glyphs with a v0.1 spacing class take their class's mean change;
   composites follow their letters.
25. Punctuation spacing. Dots (. , : ;), marks (! ?), closing and opening
   quotes, brackets and dashes shift as classes so their mean gap to neutral
   letters matches the reference; each glyph also moves halfway toward its
   class mean. Only the sides that meet letters in running text move.
26. Widths (withdrawn in v0.500, item 47). Y s T O e H get advance targets (horizontal scale with stem
   compensation, as item 12). f and t now put all of their added bar length on
   the right of the stem, so the stem no longer drifts away from the letter
   before it.
27. Semibold contrast. Curve points take y from a wght 15 heavier at
   Semibold, as item 13 does (lighter) at Light.
28. Accents. Every accented letter whose marks sit over or under the base now
   has the base letter's advance, and accented a uses the same a as plain a
   (v0.1's promotion had left some on the other design). Letters with a mark
   beside or beyond the base (d-caron, l-caron, l-dot, horned U and u, degree
   signs) keep the wider advance.
29. Kerning, computed by Everygen. Overused's kern values are removed. For
   every Latin letter, figure and common punctuation, side profiles are read
   every 10 units; the gap of a pair is the mean row gap clipped 150 units
   beyond its nearest row. A pair is kerned by 0.7 (punctuation 0.6) of its
   difference from the median gap of neutral pairs, after each side's own
   looseness is taken out, only ever closer (at most -140, punctuation -110).
   Accented and alternate glyphs share their base letter's class; classes are
   seeded from Overused's class membership and split where profiles differ;
   a glyph whose accent reaches past its base has its own class. Any pair
   whose inks would come within 30% of the lowercase gap is opened.
30. Comma family, drawn by Everygen. comma, semicolon, quotesinglbase and
   quotedblbase (and the tabular comma and semicolon) are four-point wedges
   built like the quotes of item 16: top 4 units narrower than the period, tip 0.4 of
   that, leaning left by 0.2 of the height, from the period's top to the old
   comma's bottom. The old forms stay in ss03.
31. Italic and Medium Italic. Built from Regular and Medium: sheared 10
   degrees about y=265; o O c C s S Q and zero take 2.5 degrees of that as
   rotation, are refitted to their original heights and recentred, so their
   curves keep an even weight. The single-storey a (Overused's ss01) is the
   default; ss01 gives the double-storey a. italicAngle -10, caret slope
   1000/176, fsSelection ITALIC, macStyle italic, names EverygenNeue-Italic
   and EverygenNeue-MediumItalic. Kerning is computed on the deslanted
   outlines.
32. STAT table with one wght and one ital entry per font.
33. Version 0.300 in nameID 3 and 5 and head.fontRevision.

2026-10-20  v0.201  Fix found in v0.2 verification

21. Descender kinks in y and j. The descender move (item 6, item 18) picked
   its points by testing y < -30 in each weight. In y and j one point sits
   right at -30, so it moved in some weights and not others, and where it
   stayed put the tail joined the stem with a visible kink (Regular, Medium
   and Semibold y; Medium and Semibold j). The set of moved points is now
   decided once per glyph on the wght-300 outline and used in every weight.
   Changes y, j, dotlessj, jcaron, j.ss03 and their 15 composites in
   Regular, Medium and Semibold. Light is unchanged. Version 0.201.

22. Closing quotes spacing. The drawn wedge leans left, so a letter after
   ’ or ” sat 15-32 units further away than the target gaps (median over
   a-z and A-Z, shaped with kerning). The right sidebearing of quoteright
   and quotedblright is 14 / 27 / 30 / 30 units smaller by weight. The
   median gap after them is now within 2 units of the target.

23. Quote contour direction. The drawn quotes are wound the same way as
   every other outline in the font (they were the opposite). No visual
   change.


2026-10-20  v0.200  Second Everygen build (same four weights)

Source and licence unchanged. Build script: scripts/build_v02.py, which reuses
build.py's steps and adds the changes below. Every shape is still Overused
Grotesk's own outline, transformed, except the quotes in item 16, which
Everygen drew from scratch as plain four-point wedges.

11. Weight model. The VF has wght masters at 300, 400 and 900 and no avar, so
   each coordinate is piecewise linear in wght. v0.2 takes x for each glyph
   and y for each point from their own wght on that line. Below 300 it
   extrapolates the 300-400 slope. With every option off the script
   reproduces v0.100 to within 2 units, which is rounding.

12. Individual widths (withdrawn in v0.500, item 47). W M w f X G t R K Q k C, & and @ were set to fixed
   advance targets in each weight. For f and t the parts left and right of
   the stem stretch by the same factor, so the crossbars and the hook get
   longer and the stem does not change. Every other glyph scales horizontally
   about its left edge. Its x comes from a heavier or lighter wght, chosen so
   the stem keeps its v0.1 thickness after the scale. Sidebearings keep their
   v0.1 values. Accented composites shift their marks by the base's change in
   centre and their advance by its change in width (28 composites per weight,
   Cyrillic Em, Es and Ha included). Scale factors at Regular: W 0.915,
   M 0.932, w 0.942, X 1.110, G 1.084, R 1.090, K 1.091, Q 1.062, k 1.098,
   C 1.055, & 0.967, @ 0.925. Stretch factors: f 1.32, t 1.24.

13. Light contrast. At Light, off-curve points, and on-curve points between two
   off-curve points, take y from wght 273 instead of 300. That thins the
   horizontal parts of curves and leaves straight bars and stems alone. The o
   thin/thick ratio goes from 0.95 to 0.84. The H bar stays at 53.

14. x-height. Every lowercase outline's y above the baseline is scaled by
   530/538, and the y-wght is raised (+3 to +7) so horizontals keep their
   thickness. Descenders do not move. Above-base marks on lowercase
   composites move down by the same ratio. x-height is 530 and the ascender
   is 743, down from 754. Cap height stays at 701.

15. Cap weight. Capitals take x from wght 478 at Medium (was 470) and 564 at
   Semibold (was 542). The H stem is now 100 and 121.

16. Quotes, drawn by Everygen. quoteleft, quoteright, quotesinglbase,
   quotereversed and their double forms are new four-point wedges. Each is
   wider at the top, 1.12 x stem + 8, with a tip 0.40 of that width,
   236 + 0.2 x stem tall, and leans left by 0.2 of its height. Left quotes
   are the right quote turned 180 degrees, and reversed quotes are mirrored.
   Top at 716, low quotes at 112, 42-unit sidebearings. The previous
   round-dot quotes are still in ss03.

17. Ampersand and at. & is narrowed (item 12). @ is cut from a lighter wght
   (-26 / -40 / -55 / -80 by weight), so its ring is about 12% thinner than
   the stem. It is also narrowed (item 12) and scaled 0.955 vertically about
   its centre.

18. g descender. g's tail moves down by 6, not 34. It reaches -216 next to
   p's -210, where v0.100 had -244.

19. Spacing. The Light lowercase deltas are 2 units lower per side
   (+15/+9/-1), because the wider f, t and k would otherwise push the
   lowercase average past its target. The other weights are unchanged.

20. Version 0.200 in nameID 3 and 5 and head.fontRevision.


2026-10-20  v0.100  First Everygen build (Light 300, Regular 400, Medium 500, Semibold 600)

Source: OverusedGrotesk-VF.ttf v0.6 from the Overused Grotesk repository
(one wght axis, 300-900, default 300). Build script: scripts/build.py.

1. Weight. Each static is cut from the VF with fontTools.varLib.instancer at two
   wght values. x coordinates come from the first, y coordinates from the
   second (both instances are point-compatible), which keeps horizontals a
   little lighter than verticals:
     Light     x 305 / y 290
     Regular   x 415 / y 400
     Medium    x 470 / y 450
     Semibold  x 542 / y 515
   Advance widths and kerning come from the x instance.

2. Scale. Units per em changed from 2000 to 1000 with an extra uniform x1.0769
   (total factor 7/13), so the cap height is 701 and the x-height 538. Outlines,
   advances, GPOS values and OS/2 / post metrics all scaled by
   fontTools.ttLib.scaleUpem.

3. Default glyphs. Overused's own alternates became the defaults, by swapping
   outlines between the base name and the alternate name (composites follow):
     a   <- a.spur (was cv05)      spurred a; a.ss01 <-> a.spur.ss01 likewise
     R   <- R.ss05                 straight leg; also Racute, Rcaron, uni0156,
                                   Cyrillic Ya (uni042F, uni044F)
     J   <- J.ss06                 also Jcircumflex, Cyrillic Je (uni0408)
     ss03 round dots promoted for i j . , : ; ! ? quotes, ellipsis and every
     dotted or dieresis letter in the ss03 lookup.
   278 glyph names changed content. Turning ss03, ss05, ss06 or cv05 on now
   gives back the original Overused form.

4. Spacing. Each side of each simple letter or figure was classified on the
   wght-300 outline (sidebearing near 108 with a vertical segment = straight,
   near 85 with a curve at the extreme = round; n h m right side = shoulder;
   a, G, R, seven hand-set). Deltas in 1000-upm units, left/right per side:
                lc straight  lc shoulder  lc round  cap straight  cap round
     Light          +17          +11         +1         +30          +10
     Regular        +13           +6         +1         +28          +10
     Medium         +10           +4         -3         +25           +8
     Semibold        +5            0         -6         +20           +7
   Round figure sides +6. Tabular, numerator, denominator, superior/inferior
   and enclosed figures are untouched. Composites move their marks with the
   base letter's left delta. Kerning values unchanged.

5. Space. space and no-break space set to 222 / 220 / 218 / 215.

6. Descenders. In g j p q y thorn dotlessj ef (Cyrillic) and the j.ss03 and
   y.ss07 alternates, every point below y=-30 moved down by 34 units (p now
   reaches -210, g -244). Accented and Cyrillic composites follow.

7. Vertical metrics. hhea and OS/2 typo 975 / -275 / 0 with USE_TYPO_METRICS on,
   usWinAscent 1275, usWinDescent 310. sxHeight and sCapHeight remeasured.

8. Technical clean-up. Nested composites flattened (99 glyphs) and the 7
   composites using mirrored or rotated components (nine, uni030C, uni0311,
   quoteleft, quotereversed, quotedblleft, uni201F) decomposed to outlines.
   A new box .notdef drawn. The uni000A control glyph and C0/C1 cmap entries
   removed. prep (smart dropout control) and gasp added. GDEF, fvar, gvar,
   HVAR and STAT dropped (static fonts; the VF's GDEF held no classes).

9. Naming. Family "Everygen Neue", subfamilies Light / Regular / Medium /
   Semibold (typographic family in nameID 16/17), PostScript names
   EverygenNeue-<Style>, version 0.100, vendor ID EVGN, manufacturer Everygen,
   designers Bao Nguyen (RandomMaerks) and Everygen. nameID 13 holds the OFL
   short notice and nameID 14 https://openfontlicense.org. Overused's
   "RDMK" vendor ID and its family/unique/PostScript names were removed so
   nothing claims the new name is theirs; their copyright line is kept.
   Note: the VF's own nameID 0 reads 2023-2026 with an e-mail address; the
   repository LICENSE.txt reads 2023-2025, which is the line used here.

10. Web fonts. WOFF2 files are subset to Latin and Latin Extended (Basic Latin,
   Latin-1, Latin Extended-A/B, IPA schwa, spacing modifiers, combining marks,
   Latin Extended Additional incl. Vietnamese, punctuation, currency,
   letterlike, arrows, math operators, Latin Extended-C/D), all OpenType
   features kept. TTFs keep the full glyph set including Cyrillic.
